Welcome to my survey!

Introduction

Thank you for taking part in this survey. You are being invited to participate in a research study that aims to understand how people make decisions when faced with different options that vary across several characteristics.

In this survey, you will be asked to complete a series of choice tasks. Each task presents a set of treatment alternatives, and each alternative is described using a number of attributes (features). These attributes may include things like effectiveness, cost, convenience, risks, or other relevant factors. The combinations you see are hypothetical but designed to reflect realistic scenarios.

Before you go to the survey, you need to wait 1 minute so please use this time to read the introductory text.

Purpose of the study

The goal of this study is to understand which attributes matter most to people, and how they make trade-offs between them. In real-world decision-making, choices often involve balancing benefits and drawbacks. By analyzing the choices you make, we can better understand preferences and support improved decision-making in policy, healthcare, or product design.

There are no right or wrong answers—what matters is your personal preference.

How to answer the choice tasks

For each question:

  • You will see two or more alternatives, each described by the same set of attributes.
  • Carefully read all the information presented for each alternative.
  • Choose the option you prefer the most.
  • If a “None” or “No medicine” alternative is included, you may choose it if you do not prefer any of the presented options.

Please consider each choice independently. Even if options look similar across questions, the combinations of attributes may differ.

Important reminders

  • Base your decisions on your own preferences and judgment.
  • Consider all attributes shown, not just one.
  • Take your time to understand each choice before selecting your answer.

Your responses will remain confidential and will be used for research purposes only.

Thank you for your participation.
